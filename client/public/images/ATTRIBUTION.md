# Image credits

Every photo in this folder is a real photograph (or render) of real computer
cooling fans, taken from Wikimedia Commons. They are used here under the
licences listed below.

The product *names* and *brands* in this shop (Zephyr, Vortex, Tornado, Gale,
Whisper) are invented for the exercise. Some of the photos show real hardware
from real manufacturers, so the photo and the made-up brand name on the card
will not always match. That is fine for a practice project, but it is the
reason this shop should not be put in front of real customers.

## What the licences require

- **Public domain** — no conditions.
- **CC BY** — keep the author credit and name the licence.
- **CC BY-SA** — the same, and if you modify the image and publish it, the
  modified version has to carry the same licence.

The images here were resized and re-compressed (600px wide, 1200px for the
hero) but not otherwise altered. Keeping this file next to them is what
satisfies the attribution requirement.
